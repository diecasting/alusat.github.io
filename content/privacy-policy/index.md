## Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At **YUANZHONG (alusat.com)**, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by YUANZHONG and how we use it.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you fill out our contact forms, request a quote, or upload engineering drawings (STEP, DWG, PDF). This may include:

- Name and Job Title
- Contact information including email address and phone number
- Company details
- Technical project requirements and design files

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain our website.
- Process your RFQ (Request for Quote) and provide DFM analysis.
- Communicate with you regarding project updates.
- Send technical newsletters or marketing emails (only with your consent).
- Improve our manufacturing services and website functionality.

## 3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

As a technical-led manufacturer, we understand the sensitivity of your intellectual property. We implement industry-standard security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information and engineering designs. **We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your drawings to outside parties without your explicit consent.**

## 4. Cookies and Web Beacons

alusat.com uses a small number of cookies:

- **Strictly necessary cookies** — required for the site to function (for example, remembering your cookie-consent choice). These are set regardless of your analytics preference.
- **Analytics cookies** — set by Google Analytics 4 (GA4, Measurement ID `G-41EG879WCL`) **only after you accept analytics consent**. When consent is denied, no analytics cookies are written and no measurement requests are sent. We do not use advertising or marketing cookies, and we do not use "web beacons" for ad retargeting.

## 6. Consent and Your Choices

When you first visit alusat.com, a consent banner lets you choose whether to allow optional analytics:

- **Accept analytics** — stores your choice and loads Google Analytics 4 so we can measure site usage.
- **Reject optional analytics** — stores your choice; no analytics cookies are set and GA4 emits no measurement requests.
- **Change your choice** — you can reopen the banner at any time via the "Manage cookie settings" link in the site footer and switch between accepting and rejecting.

Withdrawing or rejecting consent stops analytics on **future** page visits. Events that were already transmitted before you withdrew consent cannot be retroactively deleted by this setting; to request erasure of previously collected data, contact us using the details in Section 7.
