## Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At **YUANZHONG (alusat.com)**, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by YUANZHONG and how we use it.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you fill out our contact forms, request a quote, or upload engineering drawings (STEP, DWG, PDF). This may include:

- Name and Job Title
- Contact information including email address and phone number
- Company details
- Technical project requirements and design files

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain our website.
- Process your RFQ (Request for Quote) and provide DFM analysis.
- Communicate with you regarding project updates.
- Send technical newsletters or marketing emails (only with your consent).
- Improve our manufacturing services and website functionality.

## 3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

As a technical-led manufacturer, we understand the sensitivity of your intellectual property. We implement industry-standard security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information and engineering designs. **We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your drawings to outside parties without your explicit consent.**

## 4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, alusat.com uses ‘cookies’. These cookies are used to store information including visitors’ preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users’ experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors’ browser type and/or other information.

## 5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Our Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers (such as Google Analytics or Google Ads) for more detailed information.
