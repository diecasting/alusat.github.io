## Premium Aluminum Finishing Options

#### Anodizing (Clear & Color)

**The Process:** An electrochemical process that converts the metal surface into a decorative, durable, corrosion-resistant, anodic oxide finish.

**Best For:** [Sliding shower enclosures](/aluminum-extrusion-for-sliding-showers/) and electronic housings.

#### Powder Coating

**The Process:** Applying a dry powder through an electrostatic process, followed by heat curing to form a hard "skin".

**Best For:** Outdoor architectural structures requiring custom RAL colors and UV protection.

#### Chemical Conversion

**The Process:** Also known as chromate coating, it provides an excellent base for paint and improves electrical conductivity.

**Best For:** Internal industrial parts and aerospace components made from [7075 aluminum alloys](/aluminium-7075-vs-6061/).
