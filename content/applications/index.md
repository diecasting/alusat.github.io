## Aluminum Manufacturing Applications for Industrial OEM Customers

YuanZhong Technology Co., Limited supports global OEM customers with aluminum extrusion, die casting, CNC machining and precision metal components engineered for demanding industrial applications. Our integrated manufacturing helps product teams reduce weight, improve performance and simplify assembly.

### Aluminum Die Casting Applications

High-pressure aluminum die casting produces complex, thin-wall components with excellent dimensional repeatability. Typical applications include automotive brackets, pump housings, motor frames, gearbox cases and electronic enclosures where strength and cost efficiency matter.

### CNC Machining Applications

Precision CNC machining turns extruded profiles and cast blanks into finished functional parts. Common CNC applications cover mounting plates, heat sinks, sensor housings, hydraulic blocks and customized fixtures requiring tight tolerances.

### Precision Metal Components

YuanZhong Technology Co., Limited supplies precision metal components combining extrusion, machining and surface finishing in one controlled process. This integrated approach reduces handling, shortens lead time and improves traceability for industrial buyers.

### Automotive Applications

Lightweight aluminum structures help vehicle programs reduce mass and improve efficiency. Applications include structural brackets, battery enclosures, seating components, lighting housings and thermal management parts.

### Industrial Equipment Applications

Automation systems, machinery frames and material-handling equipment benefit from rigid yet lightweight aluminum profiles and machined parts designed for continuous operation.

### Consumer Products Applications

Aluminum's appearance, corrosion resistance and feel make it ideal for consumer electronics, appliances, lighting and sporting equipment where finish quality is critical.
