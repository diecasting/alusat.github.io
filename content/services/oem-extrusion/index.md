## OEM Aluminum Extrusion Partner

Alusat is a **dedicated OEM aluminum extrusion partner** for global manufacturers and brand owners. Rather than a transactional supplier, we behave like an extension of your engineering and supply-chain team — sharing risk early, holding the process end to end, and standing behind the parts we ship.

From the first extrusion die design to the final anodized, inspected and packed component, every operation sits under one roof and one quality system. That vertical integration is what lets us quote realistically, hold lead times and keep tolerances consistent from first prototype to high-volume production.

## Program Lifecycle Support

Alusat supports the full program lifecycle:

- **Prototypes and samples** — first-article extrusions and machined samples for fit, form and function checks before volume commitment.
- **Pilot runs** — small batches to validate finishing, packing and line integration.
- **Volume production** — recurring production with call-off scheduling against annual framework agreements.
- **New-product development** — from prototype extrusions to first-article inspection.

Because tooling, extrusion and finishing are co-located, we iterate quickly when a design changes — a real advantage for teams bringing a new product to market on a fixed timeline.
