## Cold Finished vs Extruded Aluminum

Technical Comparison of Manufacturing Processes, Mechanical Properties, and Industrial Applications.

## Performance Differences in Aluminum Manufacturing

Selecting the right material starts with understanding the manufacturing journey. As a professional [aluminum manufacturer](/aluminum-extrusion-manufacturer-in-china-a-comprehensive-guide/), we help clients navigate the choice between the speed of extrusion and the precision of cold finishing.

The core difference lies in temperature and pressure. [Aluminum extrusion](/aluminum-extrusion-process/) pushes heated metal through a die, while cold finishing pulls it at room temperature to achieve higher density and tighter tolerances.

### Key Advantage: Grain Structure

Cold working improves the aluminum's grain structure, providing a 15-20% boost in tensile strength. This is critical for aerospace and high-stress automotive parts where failure is not an option.

## Surface Finish and Secondary Processing

Surface quality is often the deciding factor. Extruded profiles may have visible lines from the die, whereas cold finished surfaces are smooth enough for immediate use in hydraulic seals.

However, both types are highly compatible with [secondary finishing](/aluminum-extrusion-finishes/). Whether you require anodizing for corrosion resistance or powder coating for aesthetics, the material's preparation dictates the final quality.
