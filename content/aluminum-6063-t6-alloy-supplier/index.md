## 6063 Aluminum Alloy: The Architect's Choice

Mastering high-precision extruded aluminum profiles with superior surface finishing and extreme corrosion resistance.

## Overview of 6063 Aluminum

[6063 aluminum alloy](/6063-aluminum-alloy/) is the gold standard for extrusion, renowned for its excellent extrudability and superb surface finish capabilities. As a leading [aluminum extrusion manufacturer](/aluminum-extrusion-manufacturer-in-china-a-comprehensive-guide/), we optimize 6063 for diverse architectural needs.

Compared to other [Al-Mg-Si alloys](/aluminum-extrusion-alloys/), 6063 is prized for its fine grain structure, which is essential for achieving a high-quality aesthetic finish after anodizing or powder coating.

### Why 6063 is Superior for Profiles

This alloy offers the perfect balance of strength and ductility. Its **T6 temper** provides robust mechanical performance while remaining easy to machine into complex [custom fabrication](/custom-aluminum-extrusion-fabrication-services/) shapes.

## Mechanical & Physical Properties

#### Mechanical (T6 Temper)

- **Tensile Strength:** 241 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 214 MPa
- **Elongation:** 12%
- **Brinell Hardness:** 73 HB

#### Material Advantages

- Excellent Surface Finish
- High Corrosion Resistance
- Good Thermal Conductivity
- Superior Extrudability

#### Standard Specs

- Density: 2.69 g/cm³
- Conductivity: 50% IACS
- Melting Point: 615°C

## Common Temper Designations

Selecting the right temper is crucial for the final application of your aluminum profiles:

| Temper | Process Details | Typical Applications |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6063-T5 | Cooled from extrusion and aged | General window frames & trims |
| 6063-T6 | Solution treated and aged | [Structural shower enclosures](/aluminum-extrusion-for-sliding-showers/) |
| 6063-T83 | Cold worked and aged | High-precision structural tubing |

## Advanced Surface Finishing

Due to its high purity and controlled composition, 6063 is the preferred material for various [surface treatments](/aluminum-surface-finishing-options/). It reacts beautifully to anodizing, creating a uniform and deep oxide layer.

Whether you need custom [heat dissipation](/aluminum-extrusion-benefits/) fins or decorative furniture trims, 6063 ensures a premium look and feel that structural alloys like [7075](/7075-aluminum/) or 6061 cannot provide.
