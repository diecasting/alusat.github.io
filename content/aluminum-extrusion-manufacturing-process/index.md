## What Is Aluminum Extrusion?

Aluminum extrusion is a manufacturing process
where heated aluminum billets are forced through
a specially designed extrusion die to create
continuous profiles with specific cross-sectional
shapes.

This process allows manufacturers to produce
lightweight, strong and complex aluminum profiles
used in industrial equipment, transportation,
architecture and many other applications.

Compared with traditional machining methods,
aluminum extrusion provides excellent material
efficiency and enables large-scale production
of customized profiles.

STEP 01

## Aluminum Billet Preparation

The aluminum extrusion process begins with
high-quality aluminum billets.

The correct alloy selection is critical because
different alloys provide different combinations
of strength, corrosion resistance, machinability
and surface quality.

- 6063 Aluminum Alloy For General Profiles
- 6061 Aluminum Alloy For Structural Applications
- 6005 And 6082 Aluminum For Industrial Requirements
- Material Quality Verification Before Production
STEP 02

## Aluminum Billet Heating Process

Before extrusion, aluminum billets are heated
to achieve the required plasticity for forming.

Controlled temperature management helps ensure
stable material flow, consistent profile quality
and reduced extrusion defects.

- Controlled Heating Temperature
- Uniform Material Softening
- Improved Extrusion Stability
STEP 03

## Aluminum Extrusion Press Process

During extrusion, the heated aluminum billet
is pushed through a precision-designed die
under high pressure.

The die opening determines the final cross-section
of the aluminum profile, allowing manufacturers
to create customized shapes for different
applications.

- Custom Extrusion Die Design
- Complex Profile Manufacturing
- Continuous Profile Production
- Dimensional Accuracy Control
STEP 04

## Aluminum Extrusion Cooling Process

After leaving the extrusion die,
the aluminum profile enters a controlled
cooling process to stabilize the shape
and mechanical properties.

Proper cooling management is important for
maintaining dimensional accuracy, surface quality
and profile consistency.

- Air Cooling Control
- Profile Shape Stability
- Reduced Internal Stress
- Improved Dimensional Accuracy
STEP 05

## Aluminum Profile Stretching And Straightening

During extrusion, aluminum profiles may experience
minor deformation due to thermal stress.

Stretching removes residual stress and improves
straightness before further processing.

- Profile Straightness Improvement
- Stress Relief
- Better Dimensional Control
- Preparation For Precision Cutting
STEP 06

## Aluminum Extrusion Cutting Process

After stretching, aluminum profiles are cut
into specified lengths according to customer
requirements.

Precision cutting ensures accurate dimensions
for subsequent machining, assembly and finishing
operations.

- Custom Length Cutting
- Precision Saw Cutting
- Production Batch Control
- Dimensional Verification
STEP 07

## Aluminum Extrusion Heat Treatment And Aging

Heat treatment improves the mechanical properties
of aluminum profiles by increasing strength and
stability.

Different aluminum alloys require different
aging conditions to achieve the desired
performance.

- T5 Heat Treatment
- T6 Heat Treatment
- Strength Improvement
- Mechanical Property Control
