## What Are Complex Aluminum Extrusion Profiles?

Complex aluminum extrusion profiles are customized
aluminum shapes designed with advanced geometries,
multiple cavities, thin walls, tight tolerances or
integrated functional features.

### Multi-Cavity Profiles

Advanced internal structures requiring precise
extrusion die design and manufacturing control.

### Thin Wall Extrusions

Lightweight aluminum profiles requiring optimized
material flow and extrusion parameters.

### Integrated Functional Profiles

Profiles combining mounting channels,
heat dissipation structures and assembly features.
