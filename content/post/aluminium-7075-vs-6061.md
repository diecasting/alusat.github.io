## 7075 vs 6061 Aluminum: Which Alloy Should You Choose?

The choice between 7075 and 6061 aluminum alloys can significantly impact your project's performance and cost.
As a leading [custom aluminum extrusion manufacturer](/),
YUANZHONG examines their mechanical properties to help you make the right selection.

## Fundamental Differences at a Glance

| Property | 7075 Aluminum | 6061 Aluminum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main Alloy Element | Zinc (5.6%) | Magnesium & Silicon |
| Tensile Strength (T6) | 83,000 psi | 45,000 psi |
| Corrosion Resistance | Fair | Excellent |
| Machinability | Good | Very Good |

## Mechanical Properties & Machining

7075 is the "super-strength" alloy of the aluminum world. It is ideal for high-stress aerospace components.

However, for parts requiring complex geometries, our [precision CNC machining service](/china-aluminum-profiles/) often recommends 6061 due to its superior surface finish and ease of fabrication.

## Surface Treatment Requirements

While 6061 has natural corrosion resistance, 7075 often requires [professional surface finishing](/aluminum-surface-finishing-anodizing-powder-coating/) such as hard-coat anodizing to perform in harsh environments.
