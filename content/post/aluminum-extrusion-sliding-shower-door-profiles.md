## Why Aluminum Extrusion is Perfect for Sliding Shower Doors

### Superior Corrosion Resistance

Specially treated aluminum extrusions withstand constant moisture. Our [surface finishing options](/aluminum-surface-finishing-options/) like high-micron anodizing provide the ultimate protection against water spots and oxidation.

### Precision Tolerance

Tight manufacturing tolerances (±0.2mm) ensure whisper-quiet sliding operation and perfect alignment. This precision is vital for the longevity of roller mechanisms and watertight seals.

### Design Flexibility

From thin-wall profiles to complex hollow shapes, extrusion allows for integrated screw ports and gasket channels. Explore our [custom fabrication services](/custom-aluminum-extrusion-fabrication-services/) for unique designs.

## Choosing the Right Alloy: 6063-T6 vs 6061-T6

The choice of alloy significantly impacts the final product's appearance and strength. For most shower applications, we recommend **6063 aluminum** due to its superior surface finish quality, which is essential for decorative anodizing and powder coating.

- **6063-T6:** The "architectural alloy." It offers an excellent balance of strength and a smooth surface. Learn more about [6063-T6 aluminum properties](/aluminum-6063-t6-alloy-supplier/).
- **6061-T6:** Used for heavy structural frames where maximum load-bearing capacity is required. See our [6061-T6 specifications](/6061-t6/).

## Key Components of Extruded Shower Systems

A high-performance sliding shower system relies on several specialized extruded profiles working in harmony:

- **Header & Sill Tracks:** Precision-engineered guides that ensure the door stays on track.
- **Wall Jambs:** Profiles that compensate for out-of-plumb walls while maintaining a watertight seal.
- **Side Lites & Glass Channels:** Securely holding tempered glass from 6mm to 12mm thickness.
- **Aesthetic Finishes:** We offer various [aluminum extrusion finishes](/aluminum-extrusion-finishes/), including brushed nickel, matte black, and bright chrome.
