## Inside Aluminum Extrusion: The Complete Manufacturing Process

Understanding the **aluminum extrusion process** is critical for engineers looking to optimize their designs for manufacturability (DFM). As an industry leader, we're detailing the exact technical steps—from raw billet to a precision [industrial aluminum profile](/china-aluminum-profiles/).

  

## The 8-Step Aluminum Extrusion Process

  

### 1. Billet Preparation

6xxx series alloys are cut and preheated to 750-900°F. For high-stress applications, choosing the right [extrusion alloy](/aluminum-extrusion-alloys/) at this stage is vital.

  

### 2. Die Preparation

H13 tool steel dies are preheated. This ensures smooth flow and prevents cracking in [precision extrusions](/precision-aluminum-extrusions-manufacturer/).

  

### 3. Extrusion Press

The billet is pushed through the die by a hydraulic ram. This is where [direct vs indirect extrusion](/aluminum-extrusion-process/) methods differ in efficiency.

  

### 4. Profile Emergence

The shape emerges and is supported by a run-out table. Surface quality is monitored here for [anodizing-ready](/aluminum-extrusion-finishes/) standards.

  

### 5. Quenching

Rapid cooling (water or air) locks in mechanical properties. This determines if you achieve a \*\*T5\*\* or \*\*T6\*\* temper.

  

### 6. Stretching

Profiles are stretched to straighten them and align the grain structure, essential for [post-extrusion machining](/aluminum-extrusion-vs-machining/) accuracy.

  

### 7. Cutting & Aging

Profiles are cut and aged in ovens. This process significantly improves the [6061 aluminum properties](/aluminum-alloy-6061-properties/) like hardness.

  

### 8. Surface Finishing

Final treatments include [powder coating or anodizing](/aluminum-surface-finishing-anodizing-powder-coating/) to protect the profile from corrosion.
