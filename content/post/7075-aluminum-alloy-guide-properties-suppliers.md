**7075 aluminum alloy** stands as a pinnacle of engineering, offering a strength-to-weight ratio that rivals many steels. ## Key Properties of 7075 Aluminum Alloy

The 7000 series is defined by zinc as its primary alloying element. For industrial applications, this creates distinct mechanical advantages:

### Superior Strength

Reaching tensile strengths of up to 572 MPa (83 ksi) in T6 temper, it is significantly stronger than 6061.

### Fatigue Resistance

Excellent endurance under cyclic loading, making it critical for aircraft wings and structural frames.

### Surface Treatment

While corrosion resistance is moderate, it responds exceptionally well to [surface treatments](/aluminum-surface-finishing-options/) for added protection.

### Heat Treatability

Highly responsive to thermal processing to achieve specific temper requirements like T6 or T73.
