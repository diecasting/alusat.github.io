## 6082-T6 Aluminum: The High-Strength Structural Alloy

6082-T6 aluminum stands as one of the highest strength alloys within the 6000 series. This alloy is the go-to material for engineers requiring a superior combination of [mechanical properties](/6082-aluminium-alloy-properties-applications-and-advantages/), weldability, and corrosion resistance. At YuanZhong Technology Co., Limited, we specialize in providing precision extrusions that leverage the unique characteristics of this versatile material.

## Key Properties of 6082-T6 Aluminum

### Mechanical Strength

Tensile strength: 310-340 MPa  
Yield strength: 260-300 MPa  
Elongation: 10-12% - Critical for structural load-bearing.

### Physical Properties

Density: 2.71 g/cm³  
Excellent thermal conductivity (170 W/m-K) for efficient heat dissipation.

### Fabrication Quality

Offers a 50% machinability rating and supports a wide range of [surface finishing options](/aluminum-extrusion-finishes/).

## Comparison: 6082-T6 vs. 6061-T6

While both are popular, 6082-T6 has largely replaced 6061-T6 in European markets due to its higher strength and better corrosion resistance in harsh environments. If you are debating between these two, check out our guide on [6061-T6 properties](/6061-t6/) for a side-by-side analysis.

| Property | 6082-T6 | 6061-T6 | 6063-T6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tensile Strength (MPa) | 310-340 | 310 | 240 |
| Fatigue Strength (MPa) | 140 | 96.5 | 90 |
| Weldability | Excellent | Good | Excellent |

### Chemical Composition (EN 573-3 Standard)

The high manganese content (0.4-1.0%) in 6082-T6 controls the grain structure, leading to a stronger alloy than its counterparts. This composition is essential for high-performance [aluminum extrusion processes](/aluminum-extrusion-process/).
