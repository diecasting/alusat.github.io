## 6061-T6 Aluminum: The Versatile High-Strength Alloy

6061-T6 aluminum is the cornerstone of modern structural manufacturing. Renowned for its balanced [mechanical properties](/aluminum-alloy-6061-properties/), this alloy is the preferred choice for everything from aircraft components to precision-machined parts. At Alusat, we provide expert insights into why this material remains an industry standard.

## Core Specifications of 6061-T6 Aluminum

### Mechanical Performance

Tensile strength: 310 MPa (45 ksi)  
Yield strength: 276 MPa (40 ksi)  
Elongation: 12% - Ideal for load-bearing structures.

### Physical Data

Density: 2.70 g/cm³  
Melting range: 582-652°C  
Excellent thermal and electrical conductivity.

### Fabrication Excellence

Highest weldability and machinability among the 6xxx series alloys. Supports [hard coat anodizing](/aluminum-surface-finishing-options/).

## Comparative Analysis: 6061 vs. Other Alloys

Choosing the right alloy is vital for project success. While 6061-T6 offers high strength, users often compare it with 6063 for decorative purposes. Explore our in-depth comparison on [6063-T6 aluminum features](/aluminium-6063-t6-alloy-supplier/) to see which fits your extrusion needs.

| Property | 6061-T6 | 6063-T6 | 6082-T6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tensile Strength (MPa) | 310 | 240 | 310-340 |
| Yield Strength (MPa) | 276 | 215 | 260-300 |
| Machinability | Good | Fair | Good |

### Chemical Composition Breakdown

- **Magnesium (Mg):** 0.8-1.2% - Enhances strength and corrosion resistance.
- **Silicon (Si):** 0.4-0.8% - Reduces melting point and improves flow.
- **Chromium (Cr):** 0.04-0.35% - Controls grain structure.
