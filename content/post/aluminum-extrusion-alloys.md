## Aluminum Extrusion Alloys: Selecting the Right Material

Choosing the optimal aluminum alloy is critical for successful extrusion projects. This guide covers the most common extrusion alloys, their properties, and how to select the best material for your application requirements.

## Most Common Aluminum Extrusion Alloys

### 6061 Aluminum

**Key Features:** High strength, good corrosion resistance, excellent weldability. Learn more about [6061-T6 properties](/6061-t6/).

**Extrudability:** Good

**Typical Tempers:** T4, T6, T651

### 6063 Aluminum

**Key Features:** Excellent surface finish, good corrosion resistance. Ideal for [6063-T6 specifications](/aluminum-6063-t6-alloy-supplier/).

**Extrudability:** Excellent

**Typical Tempers:** T5, T6

### 6082 Aluminum

**Key Features:** Higher strength than 6061, good machinability. See our [6082 alloy guide](/6082-aluminium-alloy-properties-applications-and-advantages/).

**Extrudability:** Good

**Typical Tempers:** T6

## Detailed Alloy Comparison

| Property | 6061-T6 | 6063-T6 | 6082-T6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tensile Strength (MPa) | 310 | 240 | 310-340 |
| Yield Strength (MPa) | 276 | 215 | 260-300 |
| Machinability | Excellent | Good | Good |
| Weldability | Excellent | Excellent | Good |
| Corrosion Resistance | Excellent | Excellent | Excellent |

For more technical details, you can explore the [aluminum extrusion process](/aluminum-extrusion-process/) for different materials.

## How to Choose the Right Extrusion Alloy

#### Strength Requirements

For high-stress applications, consider 6082 or 6061. For moderate loads, 6063 may suffice.

#### Surface Finish Needs

6063 provides the best as-extruded finish for visible applications.

#### Post-Extrusion Processing

6061 machines better, while 6063 anodizes more uniformly.

#### Cost Considerations

6063 is typically most economical, followed by 6061 then 6082.

### Specialty Extrusion Alloys

For unique applications, consider these less common options:

- **6005A:** Improved strength over 6063 with similar extrudability
- **6101:** Optimized for electrical conductivity applications
- **6463:** Enhanced surface finish for anodizing
