## 6082 Aluminum Specifications for Buyers

6082-T6 is favored by 6082 aluminum alloy suppliers for its "Structural Alloy" status. It offers the highest strength of the 6000 series. For a deep dive into its chemistry, view our [6082 alloy properties guide](/6082-aluminium-alloy-properties-applications-and-advantages/).

- **Tensile Strength:** 310-340 MPa
- **Yield Strength:** 260-300 MPa
- **Corrosion Resistance:** Excellent, especially in marine environments.
