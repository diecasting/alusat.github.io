## Aluminum Extrusions: The Complete Guide for 2026

Aluminum extrusions are one of the most versatile and cost-effective solutions in modern manufacturing. Whether for construction or aerospace, **extruded aluminum profiles** offer unmatched strength, lightweight properties, and design flexibility.

## What Are Aluminum Extrusions?

**Aluminum extrusion** is a manufacturing process where aluminum alloy is forced through a die with a specific cross-sectional profile. This results in long, consistent shapes that can be cut to length and further processed via [CNC machining](/china-aluminum-profiles/).

Unlike [cold-finished aluminum](/cold-finished-aluminum-vs-extruded-aluminum/), the extrusion process allows for complex hollow and semi-hollow geometries that are impossible with other methods.

## Main Types of Extruded Aluminum Profiles

Selecting the right alloy is critical for performance:

- **[6061-T6 Aluminum](/6061-t6/):** The most common structural alloy with excellent weldability.
- **[6082-T6 Aluminum](/6082-t6-aluminum/):** Known for high corrosion resistance, ideal for maritime use.
- **[7075 Aluminum](/7075-aluminum/):** Ultra-high strength for aerospace components.
- **[Precision Extrusions](/precision-aluminum-extrusions-manufacturer/):** Profiles with tolerances as tight as ±0.001".

## Key Benefits of Using Extrusions

Industries transition to aluminum extrusions because they offer:

- **Design Freedom:** Complex shapes can incorporate screw slots or snap-fit joints.
- **Cost Reduction:** Significant savings compared to [full CNC machining](/aluminum-extrusion-vs-machining/) for long parts.
- **Superior Finishing:** Ready for [anodizing or powder coating](/aluminum-surface-finishing-anodizing-powder-coating/).
