## What Sets Precision Extrusions Apart?

While standard profiles are suitable for construction, precision extrusions (±0.001" to ±0.005") require advanced integration of [precision CNC machining](/china-aluminum-profiles/) and specialized die cooling techniques.

- **Material Integrity:** Using premium alloys such as [7075 vs 6061](/aluminium-7075-vs-6061/) based on stress requirements.
- **Micro-Extrusion:** Ability to produce thin-wall sections under 0.5mm.
- **Surface Quality:** High-grade [anodizing finishes](/aluminum-surface-finishing-anodizing-powder-coating/) to maintain dimensional accuracy.
