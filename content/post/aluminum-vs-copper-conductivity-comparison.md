## Aluminum vs Copper: Which is the Better Conductor?

The choice between **aluminum and copper** is a critical decision in electrical engineering and power distribution. While copper has long been the gold standard for conductivity, modern [aluminum extrusion benefits](/aluminum-extrusion-benefits/) and alloy advancements have made aluminum a dominant force in large-scale electrical infrastructure.

## Electrical Conductivity Breakdown

### Pure Copper Performance

**100% IACS** (Standard)  
58.0 MS/m at 20°C. Best for space-constrained electronics.

### Pure Aluminum Performance

**61% IACS**  
35.4 MS/m at 20°C. Ideal for weight-sensitive applications.

### Weight Efficiency

Aluminum is 30% the weight of copper. For the same ampacity, an aluminum conductor is half the weight of copper.

## Modern Solutions: AA-8000 Series Alloys

In the past, aluminum faced challenges with thermal expansion and "creep." However, the development of **AA-8000 series aluminum alloys** has revolutionized the industry. These alloys offer improved mechanical properties and are now widely used in [electrical bus bars](/aluminum-6063-t6-alloy-supplier/) and building wire systems.

### Why Engineers Choose Aluminum Today

- **Weight Savings:** Essential for overhead transmission lines to reduce tower load.
- **Corrosion Resistance:** Aluminum naturally forms a protective oxide layer. Learn about [anodizing and coatings](/aluminum-surface-finishing-options/) for electrical parts.
- **Ductility:** Easier to form into complex [extruded profiles](/aluminum-extrusions/).
